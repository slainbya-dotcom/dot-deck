Widgets

Switch each widget on or off. Widgets that are off don’t run at all.

    Layout

    Drag widgets anywhere, on any screen. Dot Deck keeps a separate layout for each screen setup and switches automatically when you plug in or unplug a monitor.

    Screen setups
      Arrange mode Snap into place Lock positions Hide all widgets

      Arrange mode outlines every widget with its name. Widgets snap to screen edges and to each other when you let go. Hold Alt (Option on a Mac) while dragging to move a whole stack of snapped widgets together.

      Look & scale

      Scale

      Makes every widget bigger or smaller, on top of your screen’s own scaling (Windows display scaling or a Retina screen), which Dot Deck already follows. Handy on a big high-resolution monitor, or a small low-resolution one. Each widget can also have its own scale, on its tab.

      The shared look

      Widgets with Match Dot Clock ticked (on their tab, under Appearance) use the Clock’s width, dot size and colours, so they line up neatly in a stack. Change the shared look on the Clock tab.
