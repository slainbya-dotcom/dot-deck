Track from

On a Mac, apps can’t simply listen to the computer’s own sound. “Default input” is usually the microphone, which shows the room. For the music itself, install a free loopback driver such as BlackHole, send your sound through it, and choose it here. The first time, macOS asks whether Dot Visualiser may use the microphone: click OK. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
