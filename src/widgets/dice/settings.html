You can also show or hide saved rolls from the tray icon's menu. On the widget: click a die to add it, right-click (or scroll down) to remove one.

Saved rolls

    Up to 8 saved rolls, 10 dice each. About 8 letters of the label fit on the widget. With Show each, the modifier is added to every die (e.g. 2d20+5 for advantage); the widget marks those rolls “EA”.
